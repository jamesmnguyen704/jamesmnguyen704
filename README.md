## About Me

> Accountant turned data scientist, now a **Finance/Operations Systems Architect** at Day & Night Solar (DNS) — a commercial solar company. I build internal operating systems that turn accounting, bank, and document data into decisions.

- Bridging **finance workflows** (QuickBooks, reconciliation, tax compliance) with **modern software** (Python, SQL, APIs, ML)
- Obsessed with **eliminating manual back-office work** through data pipelines, document intelligence, and AI-assisted automation
- Background: **Accounting → Data Science → Full-Stack Dev**
- **5+ years** in finance operations · **50+ projects** shipped · based in St. Louis, MO

### AI-Integrated Development
